Ava

Ava is a personal AI assistant for the Windows desktop. She talks with her owner by voice and chat, controls their home lighting, keeps their calendar, and gives daily briefings. Ava is a private, single-user app: it runs on its owner's own PC and is not offered to the public.

Google Calendar

When its owner connects a Google account, Ava shows that account's calendars and creates, edits and deletes events in them on the owner's request. Details are in the privacy policy.
