Privacy policy

Ava, by Swift Designz (Windhoek, Namibia) · Last updated 5 October 2026

Ava is a personal assistant app for Windows. It is used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. This policy explains what Ava does with data, in particular data from Google Calendar.

Google Calendar data

  • What Ava accesses: with the owner's permission (the Google Calendar scope), Ava reads the events in the calendars the owner has visible in Google Calendar, including birthdays and holidays, from about 60 days in the past to about a year ahead.
  • What Ava does with it: shows the events in its calendar views, reminds the owner of upcoming events, includes them in the owner's daily briefing, and creates, edits or deletes events when the owner (or Ava, at the owner's spoken or typed request) does so.
  • Where it is stored: on the owner's own PC, in a local database. Ava has no server and keeps no copy anywhere else. The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI).
  • Sharing: Ava does not sell calendar data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone, except as follows. When the owner asks Ava a question, Ava sends the request together with relevant context, which can include upcoming event titles and times, to Anthropic's Claude API, the AI service that produces Ava's answers. Anthropic processes this to answer the request (see Anthropic's privacy policy). If the owner turns on Ava's optional phone link, reminders and replies are delivered to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • Limited use: Ava's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data

Ava stores the owner's chats, settings, notes and calendar on the owner's PC. Voice is transcribed on the PC itself. If the owner turns on the desk camera, images are analysed on the PC and are not stored; a single photo is sent to Claude only when the owner asks Ava to look at something.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect Google in Ava (Calendar › Google › Disconnect): Ava deletes the sign-in token and removes the Google events from its local calendar. The events stay in Google Calendar.
  • Or revoke Ava's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Ava and deleting its data folder removes everything Ava stored.
